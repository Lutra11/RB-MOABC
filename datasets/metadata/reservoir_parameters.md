# Reservoir Engineering Parameters for the Lower Jinsha—Three Gorges Cascade

> Data sources: Wikipedia, Baike, China Three Gorges Corporation, SASAC, Ministry of Water Resources Changjiang Commission, *Engineering Science*, *Advances in Water Science*, *People's Changjiang*, *China Energy News*, Guangdong Hydropower Society, and other publicly available materials. When citing in the paper, reference the original engineering design reports or dispatch regulations. This file is a compiled summary of published parameters.

---

## 1. Cascade system overview

The lower Jinsha River—Three Gorges cascade consists of **six hydraulic complexes** in downstream order:
**Wudongde → Baihetan → Xiluodu → Xiangjiaba → Three Gorges → Gezhouba**

The first five have flood-control storage capacity and form the core of the Yangtze flood-control engineering system. This study covers the first four (lower Jinsha cascade) plus the Three Gorges Reservoir, totaling five reservoirs.

Gezhouba is a run-of-river station without flood-control storage and is generally excluded from flood-control dispatch analysis.

- Combined flood-control storage of all five reservoirs: 376.43 × 10⁸ m³
- Accounts for ~95% of the total flood-control storage on the Yangtze main stem (Ebei Bureau, 2025)
- In the 2020 flood season, 9 flood-control dispatch operations were conducted with a total intercepted flood volume of 362.40 × 10⁸ m³

## 5. Downstream flood-control cross-section (K1)

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Control cross-section | Yichang station (~38 km downstream of Three Gorges) | — |
| Safe discharge | 56700 m³/s (1000-year design) | *Engineering Science* |
| Catchment area | 100 × 10⁴ km² | — |
| Mean annual flow | 14300 m³/s | — |
| Record period | 1877–present | — |

> The Yichang station is the final control cross-section where upper Yangtze floods enter the middle-lower plain. The Three Gorges flood-control dispatch objective is to ensure that Yichang discharge does not exceed the safe discharge. In this study, K1 is set to the Yichang cross-section with safe discharge Q̄_K1 = 56700 m³/s (1000-year design).
